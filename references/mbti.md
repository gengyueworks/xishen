# MBTI 16 型速查 / MBTI 16-Type Quick Reference

多维亮点挖掘时使用：用户给了 MBTI 类型，就从这里调取该类型的优势、盲区与成长建议，配合八字一起讲。Use this when mining multi-dimension highlights: when the user offers an MBTI type, pull that type's strengths, blind spots, and growth moves from here, and weave them together with the bazi reading.

## 使用规则 / Usage Rules

- 用户给了实际类型，就用实际类型讲。If the user gives an exact type, read that type.
- 只给范围（如 INFJ / INFP）：讲两者共同的优势，差异点用“如果你更偏 X，那……”带过。If only a range is given (e.g. INFJ / INFP): talk about what both share, and cover the differences with "if you lean more toward X, then…".
- 只给性格自述：推断 1-2 个最可能的类型，标注“我猜你可能是 X 或 Y”并说明判断依据，欢迎纠正。If only a self-description is given: infer one or two most likely types, flag it ("I'd guess you're probably X or Y"), state your reasoning, and welcome correction.
- 没有信息不硬猜。Never guess when there is no information.
- 所有推断都要配合八字印证，不单独下结论。Every inference must be corroborated by the bazi — never conclude from MBTI alone.

## 四维说明 / The Four Dimensions

- E / I：外向——从与人相处中充电；内向——从独处中充电。Extraversion — recharged by being with people; Introversion — recharged by solitude.
- S / N：实感——信眼睛看到的细节；直觉——信脑中的模式与可能。Sensing — trusts visible details; Intuition — trusts patterns and possibilities.
- T / F：思考——先讲逻辑对不对；情感——先问人心受不受。Thinking — logic first; Feeling — people first.
- J / P：判断——喜欢有安排、有结论；知觉——喜欢留余地、随变化。Judging — likes plans and closure; Perceiving — likes options and flow.

## 16 型速查 / The 16 Types

### INTJ · 建筑师 · The Architect

**核心优势 Core strengths**：战略眼光极强，能看透系统的本质，为在乎的事定下长远方向。 / Sharp strategic vision — you see how systems really work and set a long-term direction for what matters to you.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：容易觉得别人跟不上你的思路，宁可自己扛，渐渐活成独行侠。 / You can assume others can't keep up with your thinking and end up carrying everything alone.

**成长建议 Growth move**：每周挑一件正在做的事，用大白话讲给一个人听，练习把想法带人同行。 / Once a week, explain one thing you're working on in plain words to someone — practice bringing people along with your ideas.

### INTP · 逻辑学家 · The Logician

**核心优势 Core strengths**：拆解问题的天赋，凡事要摸到原理才算数，天生爱思考。 / A gift for taking problems apart — you won't rest until you reach the principle underneath, and you genuinely love to think.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：想得越多越不愿动手，容易停在“我还没想明白”。 / The more you think, the less you act — you can stall on "I haven't figured it out yet".

**成长建议 Growth move**：给想法设一个“最小版本”截止时间，先做出粗糙的一版再迭代。 / Give each idea a "minimum viable version" deadline — make a rough first pass, then iterate.

### ENTJ · 指挥官 · The Commander

**核心优势 Core strengths**：天生的组织者，能把人聚起来朝目标走，果断有担当。 / A born organizer — you rally people toward a goal, decisive and accountable.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：节奏太赶时容易压过别人的声音，让团队替你憋着不说。 / When you're in a hurry you can steamroll others' voices, and the team stops speaking up.

**成长建议 Growth move**：每次布置任务后多问一句“你怎么看”，把“听”也当成日程的一部分。 / After every assignment, ask "what do you think?" — make listening part of the schedule.

### ENTP · 辩论家 · The Debater

**核心优势 Core strengths**：点子机器，看问题总能看到别人没看到的角度，破局靠它。 / An idea machine — you always spot the angle nobody else sees; that's what cracks problems open.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：兴趣来得快去得也快，容易“开了很多头，收不了尾”。 / Interests flare and fade fast — you start many things and finish few.

**成长建议 Growth move**：一个时间段只允许自己推进一个点子，给它设“收尾检查点”。 / Allow yourself one active idea per period, and give it a "wrap-up checkpoint".

### INFJ · 提倡者 · The Advocate

**核心优势 Core strengths**：极深的共情与洞察，能读懂人心里没说出的话，温暖而有远见。 / Deep empathy and insight — you read the words people don't say, warm and farsighted at once.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：太在意别人的感受，容易把自己耗干，还不好意思说累。 / So attuned to others' feelings that you run yourself dry, and won't admit you're tired.

**成长建议 Growth move**：每天留 20 分钟“只属于自己”的时间，先照顾好自己的杯子。 / Keep 20 minutes a day that belong only to you — fill your own cup first.

### INFP · 调停者 · The Mediator

**核心优势 Core strengths**：内心世界丰盈，想象力与共情并存，能把温柔变成作品。 / A rich inner world where imagination and empathy meet — you turn gentleness into something real.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：理想太高，现实跟不上时容易自我怀疑、反复内耗。 / Ideals run so high that reality can trigger self-doubt and endless rumination.

**成长建议 Growth move**：把大理想拆成“今天能做完的一小件”，完成它，再抬头看远方。 / Break the big dream into one small thing you can finish today — finish it, then look up again.

### ENFJ · 主人公 · The Protagonist

**核心优势 Core strengths**：天生的教练与鼓舞者，能看见每个人的光，并带着大家一起发光。 / A natural coach and inspirer — you see the light in everyone and bring it out together.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：把别人的需要排在自己前面，帮完所有人却忘了自己。 / You rank everyone's needs above your own and forget yourself helping them all.

**成长建议 Growth move**：说“不”也是对他人的诚实，每周至少一次，把自己的需求放到桌面上。 / Saying "no" is honesty too — put your own needs on the table at least once a week.

### ENFP · 竞选者 · The Campaigner

**核心优势 Core strengths**：热情与感染力满格，能点燃气氛，把平淡的日子过成故事。 / Full-tank enthusiasm and charm — you light up a room and turn plain days into stories.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：兴趣点太多，注意力容易被新东西勾走，事情做到一半就换台。 / Too many sparks pull your attention — you switch channels halfway through.

**成长建议 Growth move**：用“先做 25 分钟再决定”代替“先想清楚再做”，让行动带路。 / Replace "think it through first" with "do 25 minutes, then decide" — let action lead.

### ISTJ · 物流师 · The Logistician

**核心优势 Core strengths**：可靠、守约、踏实，交给他的事就像上了保险，稳。 / Reliable, faithful, and steady — things entrusted to you are as safe as insured.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：太信熟悉的老办法，变化来时容易反应慢半拍。 / You trust the familiar way so much that you react a beat too slow when things change.

**成长建议 Growth move**：每个月主动试一件小变化，比如换条路线、换个流程，练适应力。 / Deliberately try one small change a month — a new route, a new process — to train adaptability.

### ISFJ · 守卫者 · The Defender

**核心优势 Core strengths**：细腻、体贴、记得住每个人的小事，是团队里最暖的依靠。 / Attentive, caring, and you remember everyone's small things — the warmest pillar of any group.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：习惯照顾人却不太会提要求，委屈攒久了会悄悄累。 / You're used to caring for others but rarely ask for anything — grievances pile up into quiet exhaustion.

**成长建议 Growth move**：把自己也列进“要照顾的人”名单，累了就直说，不硬撑。 / Put yourself on the "people to take care of" list — say when you're tired, don't tough it out.

### ESTJ · 总经理 · The Executive

**核心优势 Core strengths**：天生的管理者，讲规则、重效率，能把一团乱麻理出秩序。 / A born manager — rules and efficiency are your language; you turn chaos into order.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：认为“对事不对人”就是全部，容易忽略人心里的温度。 / You believe "it's not personal, it's business" covers everything, and can miss the warmth in people.

**成长建议 Growth move**：批评前先讲一句对方做得好的地方，让规则带上人情味。 / Before criticizing, say one thing they did well — let the rules carry some human warmth.

### ESFJ · 执政官 · The Consul

**核心优势 Core strengths**：热心、周到、善于凝聚关系，是朋友圈里的粘合剂。 / Warm, considerate, and great at holding people together — you're the glue of your circle.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：太在乎“大家怎么看”，容易为了合群丢掉自己的判断。 / You care so much about "what everyone thinks" that you can drop your own judgment to fit in.

**成长建议 Growth move**：做决定前先问自己一句“这是我想要的，还是别人期待的”。 / Before deciding, ask yourself: "Is this what I want, or what others expect?"

### ISTP · 鉴赏家 · The Virtuoso

**核心优势 Core strengths**：动手能力极强，临场应变快，复杂工具一到手就明白。 / Exceptionally hands-on with lightning reflexes — complex tools make sense the moment you touch them.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：喜欢独自解决，遇到情绪话题容易直接绕开，显得冷淡。 / You prefer solving things alone and dodge emotional topics, which can read as cold.

**成长建议 Growth move**：别人讲感受时，不急着给方案，先回应一句“我听到了”。 / When someone shares feelings, don't jump to solutions — first reply "I hear you".

### ISFP · 探险家 · The Adventurer

**核心优势 Core strengths**：审美与感知力出众，安静地做自己的事，作品里有自己的味道。 / Refined taste and perception — you work quietly, and your craft carries your own flavor.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：不擅长争抢，机会来的时候容易退到后面，等别人发现。 / Not one to push yourself forward — when opportunity comes you step back and wait to be noticed.

**成长建议 Growth move**：每月主动展示一次自己的作品，让好作品替你开口。 / Show your work on purpose once a month — let the work speak for you.

### ESTP · 企业家 · The Entrepreneur

**核心优势 Core strengths**：行动派中的行动派，反应快、胆大、能抓住当下，越动越有精神。 / The doer's doer — fast, bold, and present; the more you move, the more alive you feel.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：静不下来，风险意识不足，容易把“先干了再说”变成“先撞了再说”。 / You can't sit still and underweight risk — "act first" can become "crash first".

**成长建议 Growth move**：冲动出手前给自己 10 分钟，把“最坏会怎样”写在纸上再决定。 / Before an impulsive move, give yourself 10 minutes and write down the worst case before deciding.

### ESFP · 表演者 · The Entertainer

**核心优势 Core strengths**：天生的气氛担当，乐观、有人缘，走到哪都能把场子点亮。 / A born mood-lifter — optimistic and well-liked; you light up wherever you go.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：讨厌枯燥与等待，重复性的事容易烦躁，三天热度。 / You hate dullness and waiting; repetitive tasks irritate you and enthusiasm runs hot then cold.

**成长建议 Growth move**：把枯燥任务拆成小段，每段之间给自己一个“好玩奖励”。 / Break boring tasks into small chunks and give yourself a fun reward between each.
