# 十二星座速查 / Zodiac Quick Reference

多维亮点挖掘时使用：用户提供了太阳星座（或出生月日），从这里取该星座的天赋、盲区与成长建议，配合八字一起讲。Use this when mining multi-dimension highlights: when the user gives a sun sign (or birth month and day), pull that sign's gifts, blind spots, and growth moves from here, and weave them together with the bazi reading.

## 使用规则 / Usage Rules

- 太阳星座按出生月日查表（日期范围见下）。Find the sun sign from the birth month and day (date ranges below).
- 出生日在两个星座交界（前后一两天）不确定时，以占星通用分界为准，并标注“按通用分界，你算 X 座”。If the birth date falls on a cusp (a day or two around a boundary) and you're unsure, use the standard astrological boundary and note "by the usual boundary, you'd be X".
- 一切推断都要标注不确定。Flag every inference as uncertain.
- 星座内容只谈性情与天赋，配合八字印证使用，不单独下结论。Zodiac content covers temperament and gifts only — always corroborate with the bazi, never conclude from it alone.
- **禁止编造具体行星位置、相位或星象数据。** **Never fabricate planetary positions, aspects, or astrological data.**

## 十二星座 / The Twelve Signs

### 白羊座 Aries（3月21日 – 4月19日 · Mar 21 – Apr 19）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：敢想敢冲的开拓精神，天生带头，把“不可能”先踢开再谈。 / Bold pioneering drive — a natural starter who kicks "impossible" out of the way first.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：冲劲上来收不住，容易因为急而误伤身边人，也容易三分钟热度。 / When the rush hits you can't slow down — haste may bruise people around you, and enthusiasm burns out fast.

**成长建议 Growth move**：大事冲动前先睡一觉再决定，把“慢 24 小时”当成自己的护身符。 / Before big impulsive moves, sleep on it — make "wait 24 hours" your lucky charm.

### 金牛座 Taurus（4月20日 – 5月20日 · Apr 20 – May 20）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：踏实、耐得住、品味稳定，能把一件事慢慢做深做牢。 / Steady, patient, with stable taste — you take one thing and make it deep and solid.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：太舒服就不想动，改变来得慢，容易把“稳”过成“僵”。 / So comfortable you don't want to move; change comes slowly and "steady" can turn into "stuck".

**成长建议 Growth move**：每个月给生活安排一件新鲜事，从最小的一件开始，让改变不那么可怕。 / Give your life one new thing a month, starting tiny, so change stops feeling scary.

### 双子座 Gemini（5月21日 – 6月21日 · May 21 – Jun 21）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：表达与好奇心双在线，学得快、聊得开，一个人就是一支信息小分队。 / Curiosity and communication in top gear — you learn fast, talk well, and are a one-person info squad.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：兴趣太多，注意力分散，容易知道很多却样样不深。 / Too many interests pull you apart — you know a little of everything and master little.

**成长建议 Growth move**：这个月选一个主题深入到底，把“广泛”暂时让位给“深入”。 / Pick one topic and go deep this month — let depth borrow the stage from breadth.

### 巨蟹座 Cancer（6月22日 – 7月22日 · Jun 22 – Jul 22）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：情感细腻、护家护人，记得住每一份善意，直觉很准。 / Deeply feeling, protective, and loyal — you remember every kindness and your intuition is sharp.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：情绪像潮水，容易想太多，把别人的话反复在心里翻来覆去。 / Emotions run like tides — you overthink and replay others' words in your head.

**成长建议 Growth move**：情绪上来时先写下来再回应，给“想法”和“事实”之间留一道门。 / When feelings surge, write them down before replying — put a door between "what I think" and "what happened".

### 狮子座 Leo（7月23日 – 8月22日 · Jul 23 – Aug 22）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：天生的舞台中心，大方、有感染力，能把平凡日子过成庆典。 / Born for center stage — warm, magnetic, and you turn ordinary days into celebrations.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：太在意被看见，偶尔听不进逆耳的话，面子比里子重要。 / So focused on being seen that praise matters more than truth, and criticism stings.

**成长建议 Growth move**：主动把聚光灯让给别人一次，你会发现照亮别人也很快乐。 / Hand the spotlight to someone else on purpose — lighting others up is its own joy.

### 处女座 Virgo（8月23日 – 9月22日 · Aug 23 – Sep 22）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：精细、靠谱、追求把事情做到最好，是团队里的质检担当。 / Precise, reliable, and devoted to doing things well — the quality keeper of any team.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：对自己太苛刻，标准高到把自己累垮，小事也容易纠结。 / Your own harshest critic — standards so high they wear you out over small things.

**成长建议 Growth move**：每天完成一件“够好就好”的事，练习和“不完美”和平共处。 / Finish one thing at "good enough" every day — practice making peace with imperfect.

### 天秤座 Libra（9月23日 – 10月23日 · Sep 23 – Oct 23）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：天生的美感与平衡感，会说话、会协调，让人舒服是本能。 / Natural grace and balance — you speak well, harmonize others, and comfort people by instinct.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：太怕得罪人，选择困难，常常为了“大家开心”委屈自己。 / So afraid of offending that choices get hard, and you bend yourself to keep everyone happy.

**成长建议 Growth move**：小决定给自己限时 3 分钟，大决定先写下自己的第一感觉再做。 / Time-box small decisions to 3 minutes; for big ones, write down your gut feeling first.

### 天蝎座 Scorpio（10月24日 – 11月22日 · Oct 24 – Nov 22）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：专注、深刻、洞察力惊人，认定的事能潜得很深。 / Focused, deep, with startling insight — once committed, you dive to the bottom.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：爱憎分明，受伤后容易记很久，防备心把自己也关起来了。 / Love and hate run strong; old wounds linger, and the guard you build walls you in too.

**成长建议 Growth move**：试着把一件小事“当场说开”，你会发现放下比想象中轻。 / Try clearing one small thing on the spot — you'll find letting go is lighter than you think.

### 射手座 Sagittarius（11月23日 – 12月21日 · Nov 23 – Dec 21）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：乐观、自由、看得到远方，是团队里的阳光与指南针。 / Optimistic, free-spirited, and farsighted — the sunshine and compass of any group.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：讨厌束缚和细节，承诺容易被“下一个远方”带跑。 / Restless under rules and details; a new horizon can carry you away from your promises.

**成长建议 Growth move**：出发远方前，先把眼前这一件答应好的事做完，自由才踏实。 / Before chasing the next horizon, finish the one thing you promised — freedom lands better that way.

### 摩羯座 Capricorn（12月22日 – 1月19日 · Dec 22 – Jan 19）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：耐力与责任感顶级，目标感强，能把冷板凳坐穿。 / Top-tier endurance and responsibility — strong sense of purpose, willing to sit out the long game.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：太紧绷，把“休息”当偷懒，成就到手了却忘了享受。 / So driven that rest feels like laziness — you reach the goal but forget to enjoy it.

**成长建议 Growth move**：把“休息”写进日程，像开会一样认真对待，它也是计划的一部分。 / Put "rest" on the calendar and treat it as seriously as a meeting — it's part of the plan.

### 水瓶座 Aquarius（1月20日 – 2月18日 · Jan 20 – Feb 18）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：独立、前卫、点子独特，总能看到十年后的方向。 / Independent, forward-thinking, with original ideas — you spot the direction ten years out.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：抽离感太强，理性过头时，身边的人觉得你“近不了”。 / So detached and rational at times that people feel they can't get close.

**成长建议 Growth move**：每周主动分享一次真实的感受，让理性偶尔让位给温度。 / Share one real feeling a week — let warmth take the wheel from logic now and then.

### 双鱼座 Pisces（2月19日 – 3月20日 · Feb 19 – Mar 20）

**核心天赋 Core strengths**：想象力与共情力爆棚，能感受到别人感受不到的美与情绪。 / Boundless imagination and empathy — you feel beauty and emotion others miss.

**典型盲区 Blind spot**：容易把别人的情绪背在自己身上，界限模糊，累了自己。 / You carry others' emotions on your back; boundaries blur and you tire yourself out.

**成长建议 Growth move**：每天留一点“独处充电”时间，分清“他的情绪”和“我的情绪”。 / Carve out alone-time to recharge every day, and sort "their feelings" from "yours".

## 运势问题怎么答 / Answering "What's my luck" Questions

用户问“这周 / 这个月 / 今年运势怎么样”时：以八字大运流年为时间骨架，星座只给方向与语气（如“这个月适合把想象力用在创作上”）。**禁止编造具体行星位置、相位或星象数据**——查不到就说不查星象，只谈星座性情。娱乐向，最后一定落回一句温暖、可行动的建议。

When the user asks "how's my week / month / year going": use the bazi's luck periods and annual cycles as the time skeleton; the zodiac only supplies direction and tone (e.g. "this month is good for putting your imagination into creative work"). **Never fabricate planetary positions, aspects, or astrological data** — if you can't look it up, say you're not reading transits, only the sign's nature. Entertainment-oriented, and always land on one warm, actionable suggestion.
